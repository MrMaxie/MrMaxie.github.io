Daedalian Key Assist makes The Daedalian Keys easier to complete <mark>without skipping the quest</mark>. It guides you to the nearest remaining key and handles the timing challenge when a key reaches its cabinet.

## Finding the next key

Guides you to the **nearest remaining target**, whether it is a flying key or a key waiting at its cabinet. Adds the standard HUD and map markers, distance indicator, and Charmed Compass route.

Shows the nearest key's localized location when you view the quest while preserving the normal **X/16 progress**.

## Cabinet timing

Automatically performs the successful slap after you start the cabinet minigame and the key lines up with the slot.

## How to use

1. Unlock The Daedalian Keys and track the quest.
2. Follow the marker or Charmed Compass route to the indicated key or cabinet.
3. Chase the key to its cabinet if necessary.
4. Start the cabinet minigame normally. The mod waits for the key to line up and handles the successful slap.

**No additional prompt or button press** is required after the minigame begins.

## What stays unchanged

You still travel to each target, chase flying keys, and start every cabinet encounter yourself.

Quest requirements, progress, rewards, and cabinet locations remain unchanged.
