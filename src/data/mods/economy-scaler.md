Economy Scaler gives you control over <mark>money drops and shop prices</mark> in Terraria. You can change how quickly you earn coins independently of how much you spend at NPC shops.

## Earning coins

The **Money Drop Multiplier** changes the amount of money dropped by defeated enemies. It applies globally to all enemies and supports values from **0.001x to 10000x**.

## Buying from shops

The **Item Price Multiplier** uses the same **0.001x to 10000x** range. Prices are calculated dynamically when you interact with NPC shops, allowing you to make purchases cheaper or more expensive without changing enemy drops.

Configuration values are validated to keep both multipliers within their supported ranges.
