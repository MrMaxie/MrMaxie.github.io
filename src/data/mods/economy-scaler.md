Economy Scaler gives you control over <mark>money drops and shop prices</mark> in Terraria. You can change how quickly you earn coins independently of how much you spend at NPC shops.

## Earning coins

The **Money Drop Multiplier** changes the amount of money dropped by defeated enemies, from **0x to 10000x**. Set a separate multiplier for individual vanilla or modded enemies, or use **No Money Drops** to stop enemy coin drops entirely.

## Buying from shops

The **Item Price Multiplier** sets NPC shop purchase prices from **0x to 10000x**. **Free Items in Shops** makes those purchases free. Shop settings affect purchase prices only; selling prices, reforging costs, and item values stay unchanged.

Settings can be changed without reloading mods.
