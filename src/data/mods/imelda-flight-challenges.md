Easier Imelda Flight Challenges makes Imelda's broom missions and trials <mark>faster and more forgiving</mark> without changing their objectives or rewards.

## Speed and steering

Increases maximum broom speed by **35%**. Makes steering **50% more responsive**. Keeps boost available while you hold the boost control.

## Rings and boost bubbles

- **Rings:** enlarges visible rings by 20% and their pass area by 30%.
- **Boost bubbles:** enlarges boost bubbles by 10% and their collection area by 20%.

## What stays unchanged

The assistance activates <mark>only during Imelda activities</mark>. Regular free-roam broom flight remains unchanged.

Timers, scoring, rewards, course order, ring placement, and completion rules remain unchanged.
