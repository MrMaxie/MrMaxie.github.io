Boss Scaler lets you tune <mark>boss health and damage</mark> in Terraria. It detects vanilla and modded bosses and can scale their minions, including minions spawned by other boss minions.

## Tune each fight

The **damage multiplier** ranges from **0.1x to 10x**. The **health multiplier** ranges from **0.1x to 20x**, so you can adjust how long a fight lasts independently of how hard a boss hits.

**Minion Scaling Strength** sets how strongly boss multipliers affect minions, from **0% to 100%**. Boss health and damage are scaled once when an enemy spawns, so changes to settings take effect the next time that enemy spawns.

## Which enemies are included

Automatic detection uses boss and spawning information from Terraria and content mods. For enemies that need a different classification, **Enemy Scaling Rules** let you mark individual vanilla or modded enemies as **Boss**, **Minion**, or **Ignore**.
