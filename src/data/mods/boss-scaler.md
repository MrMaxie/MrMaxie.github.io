Boss Scaler lets you tune <mark>boss health and damage</mark> in Terraria. Separate multipliers make fights easier or more demanding, with scaling that also covers boss-specific minions and related enemies.

## Tune each fight

The **damage multiplier** ranges from **0.1x to 10x**. The **health multiplier** ranges from **0.1x to 20x**, so you can adjust how long a fight lasts independently of how hard a boss hits.

Related minions scale at **half the multiplier value**, keeping their strength tied to the boss without applying the full adjustment.

## Which enemies are included

Boss minions and enemies associated with boss fights are identified through **manually curated lists**. These lists may miss an enemy or include an incorrect assignment; reports through the linked Steam Workshop page help identify those gaps.
