I worked on a recruitment platform designed for a <mark>highly demanding professional market</mark>, where the quality, completeness and clarity of candidate data directly affect every decision. The product brings candidates, recruiters and hiring organizations into one process instead of leaving each side with a separate and incomplete view.

## A transparent process from every side

The platform makes the progression of a recruitment clear to everyone involved. Candidates can understand where they stand, recruiters can coordinate the process and hiring teams can work with the same current information. This reduces uncertainty around status, next steps and the information used to evaluate an opportunity.

- **Candidates:** follow the process and understand what is expected next.
- **Recruiters:** coordinate opportunities, professional profiles and communication.
- **Hiring teams:** review consistent information and keep decisions connected to the process.

## Data quality as a product requirement

In this market, a large database is useful only when its information can support real decisions. The workflows were shaped around <mark>structured and dependable professional data</mark>, helping participants compare relevant information without losing its context. The result is a recruitment experience that treats accuracy and transparency as core product behavior.

## How I built it

I developed the TypeScript interface and backend workflows connecting professional profiles, opportunities and the recruitment process. I worked across the product flow so that the information presented to each participant remained consistent as a recruitment moved forward.
